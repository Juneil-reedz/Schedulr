A local-first planner for events, meetings, tasks, notes and reminders, in a floral clay design. Everything stays on your own laptop.

## Install on Windows 10 or 11

1. Download **Schedulr_1.0.0_x64-setup.exe** below.
2. Double-click it. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. This appears because the app is not code-signed.
3. Open Schedulr from the Start menu and allow notifications if asked.
4. In **Settings**, turn on **Start with Windows** so reminders always run, and press **Send one** under Test notification.

The `.msi` file is the same app for people who prefer Windows Installer packages. You only need one of the two.

## Good to know

- Your data is saved in `%APPDATA%\com.schedulr.app` and is not removed when you update or uninstall.
- Closing the window keeps Schedulr in the system tray so reminders keep coming. Quit from the tray icon.
- Reminders cannot arrive while the laptop is off or asleep; anything missed is listed when Schedulr starts again.
- To move your data to another laptop, use **Settings > Your data > Back up**, then **Restore from a file** on the other laptop.
