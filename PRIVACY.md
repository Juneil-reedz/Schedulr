# Schedulr Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 27, 2026

Schedulr is a planner for Windows and Linux made by an independent developer (contact: areedzgonzales@gmail.com). This policy explains what information Schedulr handles and where it goes. In short: **your planner stays on your computer.** Schedulr has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and the developer does not collect or see your planner.

## Information stored on your computer

Everything you put into Schedulr is saved only on your own computer:

- events, meetings (including participant names and email addresses you enter), tasks, reminders and notes
- pictures and files you attach, your profile name and picture, and your background picture
- your settings, such as theme, layout, reminder and voice preferences
- automatic daily backups and any exports you make

On Windows this is in `%APPDATA%\com.schedulr.app`; on Linux in `~/.local/share/com.schedulr.app`. It is not uploaded anywhere by Schedulr. You can delete it at any time: delete items in the app, or uninstall Schedulr and delete that folder.

Pip, the talking companion, uses the speech voices built into your computer's operating system. The words Pip says are turned into sound on your computer and are not sent anywhere by Schedulr. Pip never listens: Schedulr does not use your microphone.

## When Schedulr connects to the internet

Schedulr works without an internet connection. It connects only for the features below.

**1. Checking for updates.** Schedulr checks the Schedulr release page on GitHub (github.com) for new versions, and downloads an update when you choose to install it. Like any website, GitHub receives your IP address and basic technical details of the request. No planner information is sent. GitHub's privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

**2. Weather (optional, off until you choose a place).** If you choose a place for the weather card, Schedulr sends the place name you search for, and then that place's coordinates, to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) to get the forecast. Open-Meteo receives your IP address with each request. Its terms and privacy policy: https://open-meteo.com/en/terms

**3. Feedback (only when you send it).** If you send feedback, Schedulr sends what you write in the feedback form to the developer through the Web3Forms service (web3forms.com), which delivers it to the developer's email. This includes your message, the kind of feedback, your first name from your Schedulr profile (if you set one), your email address (only if you enter it for a reply), and, if you leave that option on, the Schedulr version and your operating system. Nothing else from your planner is included. Web3Forms keeps a copy of submissions for up to three years under its own policy. If feedback cannot be sent this way, Schedulr instead opens a ready-written email in your own email app or Gmail, and you choose whether to send it. Web3Forms' privacy policy: https://web3forms.com/privacy

**4. Google Calendar copy (optional, off unless you set it up).** If you add your own Composio API key in Settings and connect your Google account, Schedulr sends an event's details to Composio (composio.dev) only when you choose to copy that event to Google Calendar: its title, description and agenda, start time and length, time zone, and location or meeting link. If you choose to send invitations, the participants' email addresses are included and Google sends them the invitations. Composio's privacy policy: https://composio.dev/privacy

Schedulr does not sell, rent or share your information with anyone else, and does not use it for advertising.

## Notifications

Reminder notifications are shown by your computer's operating system. Their text is created on your computer.

## Downloading Schedulr from the Microsoft Store

If you get Schedulr from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft may collect information about your download and use of the Store under its own privacy statement: https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement

## Children

Schedulr is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The developer only receives information that someone chooses to send as feedback.

## Feedback you send and how long it is kept

Feedback messages arrive in the developer's email inbox. They are used only to answer you and to improve Schedulr, are not shared, and are deleted from the developer's inbox on request. Web3Forms, which delivers them, keeps its own copy for up to three years (see its privacy policy above).

## Your choices and rights

- You control all your planner information on your computer and can view, change, export or delete it at any time.
- Weather, feedback and the Google Calendar copy are optional; if you do not use them, Schedulr sends nothing for them.
- To ask about, or have deleted, any feedback you sent, email areedzgonzales@gmail.com.
